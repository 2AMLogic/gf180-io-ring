## Limitation (quoted from spec/io-ring.md lines 143-148)

> Pre-silicon evidence is limited to (1) the clamp network turning on and carrying
> the intended current in simulation with the PDK's models across PVT, and (2) a
> layout that satisfies the ESD and latch-up rules the tools can check. It proves
> nothing about HBM/CDM robustness. Words such as "rated" and "qualified" are
> used in this spec only to forbid them. (Rule inherited in draft form from the
> origin, `pending: sky130-io-ring#2`.)

This record is a diode-model sweep. It does not establish any HBM or CDM
robustness figure, and it says nothing about the spread of thick-oxide MOS
devices: the diode process sections used here (`diode_typical`/`diode_ff`/
`diode_ss`) scale the diode area-current, series-resistance and capacitance
parameters only, and DR-0002 records that the 5/6 V FETs do not skew.

## Assumptions

1. **Stress current.** The spec states only 'HBM 2 kV, design target only'.
   The 1.33 A figure is the standard human-body-model arithmetic
   2 kV / 1.5 kohm = 1.333 A, taken here as a stated assumption, not a spec value.
2. **Forward-drop budget = 3.0 V.** The devices are the 6.0 V class diodes (the model
   header says '6.0V N+/Psub diode'; that is a label, not a parameter. The cards carry
   `bv = 11` (nd2ps) and `bv = 10.5` (pd2nw), which are reverse breakdown and are not
   used here). The budget is half of the 6.0 V class, leaving the other half for the
   rail clamp, which is not chosen yet. It covers the diode's forward drop only.
   Derived areas at 2.5 V and 3.5 V are tabulated so the sensitivity is visible.
3. **Geometry.** Square diode: `pj = 4*sqrt(area)`. Area swept 1e-11 .. 1e-7 m^2,
   33 points, ratio 10^(1/8). The derived area is the smallest *swept* area that meets
   the budget (no interpolation). 'none' means no swept area meets it.
4. **Model extrapolation.** 1.33 A through a model characterized at far lower currents
   is extrapolation: high injection and self-heating are not modeled, and the 'IKK too
   small' notice from ngspice's level-3 diode is expected. Treat the I-V above the
   knee current as a trend, not a measurement.
5. **Capacitance** is the sum of both clamp diodes' junction capacitance (both sit on
   the pad), from an ac solve at 1 MHz with the diodes reverse biased at 0 V and 5.5 V,
   against the spec's <= 3 pF added-capacitance target. The ac source is ideal and the
   diode sits behind a 1 ohm sense resistor, so C = -Im V(sense node) / (2*pi*f); the
   error from the 1 ohm is below 0.1 percent for these sizes. Reverse leakage at 5.5 V is
   information only; the spec sets no leakage target. Values near 5.5 pA are the
   simulator's gmin floor (1e-12 S x 5.5 V), not diode leakage: the model's saturation
   current at these areas is far below it, so read leakage as 'at or below the floor'.
